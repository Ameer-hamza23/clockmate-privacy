ClockMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

ClockMate is an offline chess clock for two players using one Android phone. The app package name is com.clockmate.app. ClockMate has no account and no ClockMate server. This policy explains what stays on your phone and what Google AdMob may process when an ad is shown.

Information stored on your device

ClockMate stores your settings and finished-game history only on the phone where you use the app. That includes time-control choices, sound and haptic preferences, theme, and the result of games that end on time. This information is not uploaded to us. A game you leave before it ends is not saved. Uninstalling ClockMate removes this local data.

Advertising

When the phone is online, ClockMate shows ads through Google AdMob. A banner may appear on Home, History, and Settings, and along the bottom of the clock while a game is running. A full-screen ad may appear once when you start the clock. Ads are not requested while the phone is offline.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, IP address, coarse location derived from the IP address, device information, and information about how you interact with an ad. They use this information to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, and to prevent fraud. ClockMate does not receive your advertising ID and does not sell personal information.

Google's policies apply to that processing: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

What we do not collect

ClockMate does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location, and does not process payments. There is no in-app purchase. Clock sounds and vibration stay on the device.

How information is shared

The only third party that receives information from use of ClockMate is Google, through AdMob, for the advertising described above. We do not sell data. Local clock settings and game history are not shared.

Children

ClockMate is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad, contact us and contact Google so the request can be reviewed.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when ClockMate changes. The current version will always be available at this page. For privacy questions, contact zenith.stack.studio@gmail.com.
